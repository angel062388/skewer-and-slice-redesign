Good To Know

Questions, Answered

Hours, pickup, and what comes on the plate. Anything else, call (719) 368-6548.

What are your hours?

Daily, 11AM to 9PM.

Where are you?

6970 Mesa Ridge Pkwy, Unit #130, Fountain, CO 80817. Get directions.

How do I order for pickup?

Use the Order Online button, or call (719) 368-6548.

Can I eat in?

Yes. Stop in for a quick bite or sit down for a full meal, whichever you are in the mood for.

What comes with a kabob plate?

Every kabob plate is served with seasoned rice, chickpeas, salad, homemade nan bread and our sauces.

What is in a wrap?

All wraps come in Afghan bread with lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, onions and feta.

Is the pizza wood-fired?

Yes. Every pie comes out of the wood oven, including the chicken shawarma pizza.

Do you have meat-free options?

Yes: the Falafel Plate (K11), the Falafel Wrap, the Veggies Pizza (P3), plus hummus, dolma and nan from the appetizers.

What is the Sunday special?

S1, Lamb Shank Qabuli Palou: slow-braised lamb shank over Afghan qabuli palau rice. Sundays only.

What is for dessert?

Baklava, cheesecake baklava, cream roll, phirni (coconut pudding), nut roll, and gelato in 4, 6 or 8 oz.
